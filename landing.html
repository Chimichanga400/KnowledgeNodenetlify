Questions students actually ask

Is the free plan actually free?

Yes — notes, spaced repetition and study plans are free forever, no card required. Premium adds the AI features (tutor, OCR, AI Director).

Will it work with my subjects?

It studies your notes, so it works for any subject and any curriculum — matric NSC/IEB, university modules, professional exams.

How do I pay?

Card or instant EFT via Paystack on the web, or through Google Play in the Android app. Cancel anytime — you keep the free features.

Does it use a lot of data?

Your notes live on your device and reviews work offline. Only AI conversations need a connection, and they're text — a few study sessions use less data than one Instagram reel.

Is my study data private?

Your notes are stored on your own device, not on our servers. AI requests are processed securely and never sold. Full privacy policy available in the app.

Can the AI guarantee better marks?

No app honestly can. What it guarantees is structured, consistent studying — spaced repetition and active recall are the two most evidence-backed study methods known.
