Questions, answered

Does it work for my subjects?

Yes — KnowledgeNode learns from your notes, so it works for any subject and any curriculum (CAPS, IEB, university modules). If you can write it down or photograph it, it can teach it back to you.

Is the AI always right?

No AI is perfect. KnowledgeNode is built to quiz and coach you on your own material, which keeps it accurate — but for anything mark-critical, always double-check against your class notes. We're honest about this because your marks matter more than our marketing.

How do I pay?

Securely in Rands with any South African card via Paystack. Your access is activated shortly after payment, and you can cancel Premium anytime.

What if it's not for me?

Start with the free 7-day trial — no card needed. If you subscribe and change your mind within 7 days of payment, we refund you in full, no questions asked.

Do you offer school or tutoring-centre licences?

Yes — bulk licences work out to under R50 per learner per month, with a free two-week pilot so you can see real results first. Use the "Book a pilot" button above.
